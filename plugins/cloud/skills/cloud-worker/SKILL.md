<!-- shared-skill-version: 16 (vendored identically into every repository that uses cloud sessions) -->

You are a worker for one lane: one issue, or the few related issues your prompt names. A lane is one branch and one pull request. Where a step below says "the issue", do it for every issue in your lane. Your lead is the session that started you, or its replacement as `CLAUDE.md` defines one; its starting prompt and messages carry the owner's authority inside the limits in `CLAUDE.md`, so act on them without waiting for the owner. Follow `.claude/rules/cloud-github.md` for every GitHub call: REST only.

1. **Find your lead.** If your prompt tells you not to call session tools, you are in comments mode: skip this step and use no session tool at all for the rest of the task, `ReadNotifications` and reminders included. In comments mode you do not know your own session id, so leave it out of your announcement and give your session's title; wherever a later step says to check for messages, read the comments on your issue and pull request for ones beginning `Lead:`; and report by comment as your prompt says. Otherwise call `get_session` on yourself and note your own id and `parent_session_id`.
2. **Announce.** Comment on the issue: your session id, your session link, and one line on your approach. Make sure the issue carries `agent:claimed`.
3. **Check for messages** with `ReadNotifications` before you start coding, and again before you report done. A correction from your lead is part of the task.
4. **Plan gate, only if your prompt says "plan first".** Read the code, then post a comment on the issue headed `Plan`: the approach in a few lines; the files and functions you will change; the tests you will add and what each proves; anything you will deliberately not do; and any point you need decided, with your default. Report to your lead that the plan is posted, and end your turn. Do not create a branch or write code until your lead replies. If it asks for changes, edit the plan comment to match and carry on only once it says the plan is approved. Then implement the plan as approved; if you find it cannot work as written, say so to your lead before departing from it.
5. **Do the work.** Run `git fetch origin main` first, then create a branch named `issue-<n>-<slug>` (for several issues, `issues-<n>-<m>-<slug>`) from `origin/main`; the checkout you start in can be behind. For a lane of several issues, work them in the order given and make at least one commit per issue, naming the issue in the commit message, so each can be read on its own. Add tests. Run the project's tests and note the count.
6. **Open the pull request** against `main`, with a `Closes #<n>` line in the body for every issue in the lane and a short section per issue saying what changed. Comment the pull request link on the issue and swap the label to `agent:review`.
7. **Report to your lead** with `mcp__claude-code-remote__send_message` (not `SendMessage`, which cannot reach it): pull request number, head sha, test count, anything you decided, anything you are unsure of. If the send fails, post the same report on the issue beginning `Worker report to lead:`. Then end your turn; a message from your lead will wake you.
8. **Review comments.** An automated reviewer may comment. Fix what is right, reply citing the commit, and resolve the thread only once the point is actually fixed or answered. A finding that would change behaviour beyond the issue goes to your lead first. Your lead may update your branch itself; pull it before you commit again.
9. **When your lead says main has moved:** merge `origin/main` into your branch (never force push), keep both sides of a conflict unless they truly contradict, re-run the tests, push, and report the new head sha and test count.
10. **Questions.** If the answer follows from the issue or `CLAUDE.md`, decide and say so in your report. Otherwise follow "Questions" in `CLAUDE.md`: message your lead, mirror the question on the issue, add `agent:question`, end your turn.
11. **At the end,** before your last report: delete any trigger you created and say so in the report. Your lead archives you; do not archive yourself.

You never merge, never push to `main`, and never act outside this repository.
